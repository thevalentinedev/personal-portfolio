FlowForce

FlowForce

Founding Engineer · flowforce.app

AI CRM for sales teams and real estate brokerages—one workspace for pipeline, dialing, cadences, and inbox outreach.

Problem

Reps juggle separate CRM, dialer, email, and sequencer tools. Half of outreach hits bad data; follow-ups go cold while admins eat most of the day.

What I built

Core product engineering: lead/deal workspace, Power & Parallel Dialer, multi-step cadences, Ask Flow (context-aware AI), and AI Agents that queue follow-ups from live signals—with enrichment and activity timelines on every record.

5× more conversations/hr with parallel dialer
10+ hrs saved per rep/week (customer-reported)
99.9% platform uptime (last 12 mo)

Outcome

Teams replace fragmented stacks with one product. Brokerages including Keller Williams use FlowForce for outbound, nurture, and AI-assisted closing workflows.
